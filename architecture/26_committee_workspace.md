# Committee Workspace

Version: 2.0  
Status: Architecture Baseline

## Purpose

The committee workspace supports lawful oversight, policy, budget, risk, and stewardship without encouraging operational micromanagement or history manipulation.

## Oversight views

Committees see governed trends for safety, recurring Incidents, service commitments, asset risk, evidence quality, control exceptions, continuity, vendor performance, and learning. Reports use versioned KPI definitions, privacy suppression, as-of time, and drill-through limited by authority.

## Reserved decisions

The workspace supports resolutions for policy, budgets, major risk acceptance, supplier appointments, authority frameworks, retention exceptions, architecture direction, and other matters reserved by bylaws or law. Each resolution records agenda context, evidence, alternatives, votes/dissent, conflicts, authority, effective date, and review trigger.

## Boundaries

Committee members do not gain unrestricted personal-data or evidence access merely by office. They do not rewrite timelines, conceal embarrassing events, or assign operational blame through dashboards. Removal requests follow legal, privacy, retention, and independent-review processes.

## Transitions

Incoming members receive current policies, risks, unresolved decisions, architecture rationale, audit findings, and stewardship obligations. Departing members lose access promptly while their historical actions remain attributed to the role and time.

## AI and analytics

AI may summarize packs and identify patterns, but citations, missing data, uncertainty, and contextual mismatch remain visible. The committee—not AI—adopts policy or risk decisions. Metrics are used for inquiry and improvement, not automatic punishment.

## Future evolution

Electronic voting or signatures may be added only when identity, quorum, legal validity, record immutability, and export requirements are satisfied.
