# Manager Workspace

Version: 2.0  
Status: Architecture Baseline

## Purpose

The manager workspace supports operational prioritization, assignment, investigation oversight, exceptions, and accountable decisions.

## Core views

The intake view shows separate Cases, safety signals, impact opinions, acknowledgement failures, and similarity suggestions without merge controls. Investigation views compare location, asset/component, symptom, cause hypotheses, repair relationship, elapsed time, and evidence before a human Incident association decision.

The work view separates Organizational Priority, due commitments, capability, availability, accepted commitments, and technician Execution Sequence. Capacity views expose conflicts without treating availability as willingness or capability.

## Decision support

Managers see observations, evidence, hypotheses, AI recommendations with citations, authority source, alternatives, and consequences before recording priority, association, override, or closure decisions. Every override identifies affected work and notifications.

## Exceptions

Queues cover safety escalation, unacknowledged reports, delayed evidence, offline synchronization, failed integrations, overdue commitments, verification failures, external work discovered after the fact, access conflicts, and challenged decisions. Emergency actions are reviewed after stabilization, not criticized merely for preceding data entry.

## Remote verification

A manager may verify remotely only with the required qualification, authority, evidence package, and disclosure of limitations. Where independent or on-site verification is mandated, the workspace prevents self-approval and routes review.

## Continuity

Handover views include current understanding, open commitments, risk, authority expiries, and unresolved decisions. Dashboards link every metric to its definition and source; analytics never replace case-level judgment.
