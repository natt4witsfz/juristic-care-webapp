# Evidence Standards

Version: 2.0  
Status: Normative Architecture Baseline

## Purpose

These standards define proportionate evidence expectations for investigation, work, and verification without placing documentation above safety.

## General standard

Evidence must be relevant, attributable, time-aware, integrity-protected, minimally necessary, and understandable in context. Reviewers distinguish authenticity, reliability, relevance, completeness, and limitations; no item automatically proves a claim.

## Risk tiers

| Tier        | Example                                        | Minimum expectation                                                                                                           |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Routine     | Low-impact adjustment                          | Technician observation, action, outcome                                                                                       |
| Significant | Repeat failure or material repair              | Before/after evidence, asset/component, method/material, independent or authorized verification                               |
| Critical    | Life safety, major property, essential service | Hazard assessment, detailed chronology, controlled evidence package, qualified independent verification and management review |

Policy may refine examples, never weaken legal requirements.

## Capture requirements

Record source, creator, capture method, event/capture time, recorded/upload time, asset/location when relevant, consent/classification, and claim relationship. Preserve originals; annotations and redactions are derived renditions. Measurements include unit, instrument, calibration context when material, and uncertainty.

## Exceptions

Danger, battery failure, privacy, access, weather, or connectivity may prevent ideal capture. Stabilization proceeds. The exception record explains the limitation and identifies alternative evidence such as witness observations, system readings, later inspection, or supplier documentation. Missing media alone must not invalidate genuine emergency work.

## Verification

The verifier records method, evidence reviewed, acceptance criteria, conflicts, limitations, and decision. Remote verification is acceptable only when risk policy and evidence sufficiency permit it. A failed check preserves both the submitted package and finding.

## Retention and disclosure

Retention follows legal and business purpose. Access and redaction protect people while preserving authoritative integrity. Chain of custody is required when evidence may support legal, insurance, safety, or disciplinary processes.
