# Governance Model

Version: 2.0  
Status: Architecture Baseline

## Purpose

Governance defines how the Juristic Person directs the system, delegates authority, manages risk, and preserves memory across leadership and supplier changes.

## Governance hierarchy

Applicable law and bylaws take precedence over committee resolutions, approved policies, procedures, delegations, and individual decisions. Each authority-bearing record cites its source and effective version. Conflicts are escalated; software does not silently choose.

## Decision rights

Residents report observations and express impact or urgency opinions. Authorized juristic managers, dispatchers, building managers, or assigners decide Organizational Priority. Technicians decide safe practical Execution Sequence within their capability and constraints. Qualified verifiers decide whether evidence satisfies acceptance criteria. Committees set policy, oversight, budgets, and reserved decisions within their mandate.

## Controls

High-impact actions use least privilege, separation of duties, reason capture, and review proportional to risk. Examples include policy change, authority delegation, evidence restriction, Incident closure, retention exception, bulk export, and AI configuration change. Emergency action is reviewed after stabilization, never blocked when delay increases harm.

## Policy lifecycle

Policies are drafted, reviewed, approved by an authorized body, published with an effective date, monitored, revised, and retired. Historical versions remain linked to actions taken under them. Retroactive policy application is prohibited unless legally required and explicitly recorded.

## Stewardship transition

Departing committees, managers, vendors, and developers must hand over open risks, active mandates, system credentials through secure processes, architecture decisions, data exports, retention obligations, and unresolved questions. No supplier or founder may make Organizational Memory inaccessible.

## Disputes and correction

Participants can challenge classification, priority, evidence interpretation, or decisions. The challenge is appended, routed to an independent authorized reviewer, and resolved by a new Decision Record. Privacy or legal restrictions may limit visibility but not secretly rewrite history.

## Assurance

Governance is reviewed through access recertification, decision sampling, retention tests, restore exercises, AI evaluation, conflict-of-interest review, and continuity drills. Findings become tracked Operations or governance decisions.
