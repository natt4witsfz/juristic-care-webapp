# O83 Care by Frostberg

## Mission

Build a practical property operation platform that enables every stakeholder to understand:

- What needs to be done
- Who is responsible
- Current progress
- What has been completed
- Why decisions were made

The system must improve transparency, preserve operational knowledge, and reduce communication gaps.

---

# Phase 1 Objective

The first production release focuses only on the core repair workflow.

The system must allow users to:

1. Report damage
2. Assign responsibility
3. Track work progress
4. Verify completion
5. Preserve Before/After evidence
6. Generate a final PDF report

Anything beyond these six capabilities belongs to a later phase unless explicitly approved.

---

# Design Principles

Clarity over Decoration.

Human First.

Transparency over Assumption.

Evidence before Opinion.

Every important action should leave a trace.

Knowledge belongs to the organization, not individuals.

---

# Long-Term Vision

O83 Care is designed to become the operational memory of the property.

Future committees, juristic managers, technicians, and staff should understand previous work without relying on verbal explanations.

The system should preserve context, history, decisions, and evidence for future generations.
