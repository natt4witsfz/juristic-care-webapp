# Project Glossary

This document defines the official terminology used throughout O83 Care.

Every AI agent, developer and contributor should use these terms consistently.

---

# Business Terms

## Case

A unit of work created to resolve a problem.

A Case has:

- Reporter
- Location
- Description
- Assignee
- Status
- Timeline
- Evidence
- Verification
- Final PDF

---

## Incident

An unexpected event affecting property operations.

One Incident may contain multiple Cases.

---

## Reporter

The person who reports a problem.

Usually:

- Resident
- Juristic Staff
- Technician

---

## Resident

Property owner, tenant or authorized occupant.

Residents can only view their own Cases.

---

## Juristic Staff

Staff responsible for receiving reports, assigning work and verifying completion.

---

## Technician

Responsible for carrying out assigned work and submitting evidence.

---

## Verification

Formal inspection confirming that work has been completed correctly.

Verification is required before closing a Case.

---

## Evidence

Objective proof supporting work.

Evidence may include:

- Before photos
- After photos
- Videos
- Measurements
- Documents

---

## Timeline

Chronological history of all important Case activities.

Timeline entries cannot be deleted.

---

## Audit Log

Permanent history of important system actions.

Audit records are immutable.

---

## Current Truth

The current operational state calculated from actual system data.

Never entered manually.

---

## Primary Owner

Main person responsible for a Case.

---

## Co-Responsible

Additional people participating in the Case.

---

## Archive

Completed Cases retained for historical reference.

---

## PDF Report

Permanent report generated after Case completion.

Represents the official record of the Case.
