	# System Rules

## General

The system must never sacrifice data integrity for convenience.

---

## Evidence

Before photos are mandatory.

After photos are mandatory.

Evidence cannot be deleted after work completion.

---

## Audit

Every important action must be recorded.

No audit record may be removed.

---

## Permissions

Residents can only see their own reports.

Technicians only see assigned work.

Managers can supervise all work.

Admins configure the system.

---

## Workflow

Status changes require actions.

No drag-and-drop status changes.

Verification must happen before closure.

Reopen must preserve history.

---

## Documentation

Every completed case generates a PDF report.

PDF becomes permanent project history.
