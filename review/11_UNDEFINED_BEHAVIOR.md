# Undefined Behavior

These items require accountable Juristic Person decisions or completed architecture refinements before their workflows can be coded:

1. Thai legal retention and erasure treatment for resident identity, communications, access, evidence, CCTV/video, audit, and committee records.
2. Exact emergency authority, duty succession, retrospective recording window, and approval/escalation requirements.
3. Committee Mandate limits, quorum, acknowledgement versus approval, recusals, and delegated authority.
4. SLA calendars, pause reasons, holidays, vendor clocks, due-date precedence, and breach escalation.
5. Required evidence by work/category, acceptable unavailability reasons, file limits, measurement units/calibration, and remote verification rules.
6. Recurrence windows and follow-up conditions by asset/component, including the water-leak rain condition.
7. Resident dispute, refusal-of-access, no-response, appeal, and communication timing rules.
8. Housekeeping Case source and data scope; the current command intentionally does not mislabel housekeeping input.
9. Vendor disappearance, substitution, commitment release, retained access, and contract/security handling.
10. Notification channels, quiet hours, life-safety overrides, language, templates, delivery evidence, and acknowledgement deadlines.
11. RPO, RTO, archive access, long-term export format, and off-platform custody for Organizational Memory.
12. AI use cases, provider/data residency, approved data scope, evaluation thresholds, model-change review, and incident disablement.

Defaults must not be silently invented. Each resolution becomes a versioned policy/decision with effective date, evidence, owner, and migration impact.
