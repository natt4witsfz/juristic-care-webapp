# O83 Care Domain Entity Catalog

Version: 1.0  
Status: Database Translation Review

## Purpose

This catalog validates every identified business object before physical database design. “Entity” is used broadly for review; the **Kind** column identifies aggregates, owned entities, value objects, roles, policies, taxonomies, events, and projections. This distinction is mandatory: not every noun should become an independently mutable table.

## Interpretation of catalog fields

- **Lifecycle/history** includes versioning and prior-state requirements.
- **Retention** combines soft-delete and archive policy. “No soft delete” means corrections or status transitions append; lawful disposition remains governed.
- **Use** combines searchability, reporting, AI use, and scale considerations.
- Cardinalities are logical and do not prescribe SQL.

## Catalog result

The review identifies 78 business and supporting objects. Forty-six are sufficiently defined for logical translation, nineteen are partially defined, and thirteen require a binding architecture decision or model extension before a production database can preserve their intended meaning. The most serious deficits are property/asset structure, cross-tenant correction, Contract/SLA details, proactive maintenance, Task semantics, and governed taxonomies.
