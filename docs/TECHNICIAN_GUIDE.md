# Technician Guide

Technicians see Operations for which their role or accepted Commitment grants access. Assignment is an offer or allocation; acceptance is a separate recorded act.

Before normal repair work, confirm location, asset/component, safety conditions, current understanding, required before evidence, and authority to proceed. Record observations separately from hypotheses. During emergencies, protect people first and document event/action/record times, reason, authority, evidence gaps, and work performed after stabilization.

Completion normally includes before/after evidence, measurements or test-video metadata when required, actions, parts, exceptions, and any follow-up condition. A technician submits completion; a qualified human verifies it. Disagreement is preserved, not hidden. If the repair fails or new evidence appears, create a new report/Case and let Investigation decide recurrence or relationship to prior work.

The current application provides read-only Operation visibility and Case/Investigation foundation. Task acceptance, evidence upload, completion, transfer, and emergency command screens remain pre-production gaps.
