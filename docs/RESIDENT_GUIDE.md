# Resident Guide

Use the Case form to describe what you observed and where. Each submission receives a separate reference so your original report remains traceable even when other residents report the same event.

For immediate danger, contact the building's emergency channel first; do not wait for the application. Avoid entering unsafe areas. Add information and evidence only when safe and lawful.

Investigation may associate your Case with a verified Incident, but your Case remains individually visible according to your relationship. Completion means the organization has submitted and verified work under its process; you may still dispute the outcome or report recurrence. A later problem becomes a new Case and is not automatically treated as the same Incident.

Resident data is protected through relationship-scoped RLS. The hosted Auth, notification, evidence, dispute, and acknowledgement flows require production provisioning and validation before resident launch.
