# Knowledge Model

Version: 2.0  
Status: Architecture Baseline

## Purpose

The Knowledge model converts experience into reusable guidance without treating historical success as universal truth.

## Knowledge Record

A Knowledge Record contains a concise claim or practice; source observations, decisions, Operations, outcomes, and failed hypotheses; author and reviewers; confidence; limitations; and validity context. Required context includes asset and component, make/model/version when relevant, location and environment, governance and workflow versions, effective period, assumptions, evidence base, last verification, and superseded status.

## Lifecycle

Draft knowledge may be proposed by a human or AI. An authorized steward reviews evidence and context, then publishes, rejects, or requests refinement. Published knowledge can be challenged, reverified, deprecated, or superseded. All versions remain in Organizational Memory.

## Reuse rules

Before reuse, the consumer—human or AI—compares the historical validity context with the current Case, Incident, asset, environment, and policy. Mismatches are shown, not hidden. Guidance with expired verification or material mismatch is labeled uncertain and cannot be presented as a proven solution.

## Distinctions

- An observation describes what was perceived or measured.
- A hypothesis proposes an explanation.
- Evidence supports or challenges a claim.
- A decision records an accountable choice.
- An outcome records what followed.
- Knowledge is a reviewed, context-bound synthesis.

## Governance and stewardship

The Juristic Person owns Knowledge Records. Contributors retain attribution but cannot privately remove organizational learning. Sensitive content may be access-restricted or redacted in projections while the authoritative record remains governed and auditable.

## Edge cases

Conflicting lessons coexist with their contexts until resolved. A successful repair with weak evidence may become a low-confidence candidate, not a best practice. Vendor advice is labeled by source and commercial interest. A superseded lesson remains discoverable when reviewing historical decisions.

## Evolution

Taxonomies and confidence scales are versioned. New machine-readable context fields may be added without invalidating narrative records. AI behavior is constrained by [07_ai_advisor_model.md](07_ai_advisor_model.md) and [29_ai_collaboration_principles.md](29_ai_collaboration_principles.md).
